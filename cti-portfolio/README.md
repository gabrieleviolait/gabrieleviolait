# Cyber Threat Intelligence & OSINT Research Portfolio

Independent research focused on cybercrime, hacktivism, ransomware/RaaS, underground ecosystems and emerging cyber threats.

This public portfolio outlines research methodologies and analytical capabilities. Detailed investigations, operational notes and supporting evidence are not published for privacy, confidentiality and operational security (OPSEC) reasons.

## Research methodologies

- Intelligence cycle: requirements, collection, processing, analysis, dissemination and feedback.
- Passive OSINT and lawfully accessible restricted-source research (CLOSINT).
- Threat actor profiling, infrastructure analysis and longitudinal monitoring.
- IOC correlation and enrichment, TTP analysis and MITRE ATT&CK mapping.
- Source validation, evidence triangulation, confidence assessment and evaluation of alternative hypotheses.
- Python-based research automation and structured intelligence reporting.

## Research scope

- Cybercrime and ransomware/RaaS ecosystems.
- Hacktivism and DDoS threat landscapes.
- Dark Web and underground ecosystem monitoring.
- Data breach monitoring and emerging threat analysis.

## Privacy and research ethics

Research is conducted within applicable legal and ethical boundaries, with attention to source protection, data minimisation and responsible disclosure. Public materials intentionally omit specific research targets, operational details, sensitive indicators and identifying evidence.
